## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Open Source Professional
**Recommended Capstone:** Linux Foundation Certified IT Associate (LFCA)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Linux Foundation |
| **Cost** | $250 |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why this certification?**
The LFCA validates your Linux fundamentals and IT skills—the foundation for any open source career. It's vendor-neutral, globally recognized, and directly prepares you for advanced certifications like LFCS (Linux Foundation Certified System Administrator). Your Coursera prep courses align perfectly with the exam objectives. This is your Shield—proof you can work in real Linux environments.
