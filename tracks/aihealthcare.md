# Track: AI Healthcare Specialist

| | |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Healthcare AI, Clinical Informatics, Medical Data Analytics, AI Ethics in Healthcare |
| **Est. Cost** | ~$1,100 |
| **Time to Complete** | ~12-18 Months |
| **Target Degree** | B.A. in Liberal Arts |

**Why:** Healthcare is being transformed by AI—from diagnostic imaging to drug discovery to clinical decision support. But healthcare AI requires understanding both the technology AND the domain: HIPAA, clinical workflows, patient safety, FDA regulations. This track builds AI literacy plus healthcare context for roles bridging medicine and machine learning.

## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### AI Healthcare Specialist
**Recommended Capstone:** AI in Healthcare Specialization (Stanford) + HIPAA Certification

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Stanford (Coursera) / Various HIPAA providers |
| **Cost** | Stanford: ~$150 / HIPAA: ~$25-50 |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why these certifications?**
Stanford's AI in Healthcare covers clinical data, medical imaging AI, and regulatory considerations—the gold standard for healthcare AI education. HIPAA certification is table stakes for any healthcare role; it shows you understand patient privacy requirements. Together, they prove you can work responsibly at the intersection of AI and healthcare. This is your Shield—proof you understand both the algorithms AND the regulations.
