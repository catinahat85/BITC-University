## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Nonprofit Management
**Recommended Capstone:** Fundraising and Development Foundations (UC Davis)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | University of California, Davis |
| **Cost** | ~$49/month (2 months = ~$98) |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why this certification?**
This UC Davis specialization covers the complete fundraising lifecycle: prospect research, donor cultivation, solicitation, and stewardship. You'll learn annual giving, major gifts, planned giving, and campaign management. Essential skills for any nonprofit leader—from executive directors to development officers. This is your Shield—proof you understand how to sustain mission-driven organizations.
