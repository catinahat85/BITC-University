## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Business Leader
**Recommended Capstone:** [Wharton Business Foundations Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/wharton-business-foundations)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | University of Pennsylvania (Wharton) |
| **Cost** | ~$49/month (2 months = ~$98) |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why this certification?**
The Wharton Business Foundations Specialization is taught by faculty from the #1-ranked business school in the world. Six courses covering Marketing, Financial Accounting, Operations Management, Corporate Finance, and Human Capital—with a capstone project applying skills to real business challenges from companies like Shazam and SnapDeal. An Ivy League credential on your resume for under $100. This is your Shield—the proof that your degree is backed by world-class business education.
