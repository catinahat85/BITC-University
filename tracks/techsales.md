# Track: Tech Sales Leader (T-Shaped)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Enterprise Sales, Solutions Architecture, Technical Pre-Sales, Sales Engineering |
| **Est. Cost** | ~$1,720 |
| **Time to Complete** | ~9-12 Months |
| **Target Degree** | B.A. in Liberal Arts, Business Administration |

**Why:** The highest-paid sales roles require technical depth. Account Executives at companies like Salesforce, AWS, and Snowflake earn $200K+ OTE because they can speak to CTOs and close seven-figure deals. Solutions Architects and Sales Engineers bridge the gap between product and customer. This "T-shaped" track builds broad business skills with deep technical credibility.

## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Tech Sales Leader
**Recommended Capstone:** Salesforce Administrator Certification + AWS Cloud Practitioner

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Salesforce / Amazon Web Services |
| **Cost** | Salesforce Admin: $200 / AWS CP: $100 |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why these certifications?**
Salesforce Admin proves you understand the #1 CRM platform inside-out—essential for any tech sales role and many Solutions Architect positions. AWS Cloud Practitioner demonstrates cloud fluency, critical for selling to technical buyers. Together, they signal you can work a deal in Salesforce AND whiteboard a solution with a CTO. This is your Shield—proof you're not just a quota-carrier but a technical partner.
