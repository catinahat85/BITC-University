## The Platform Builder's Playbook

1. **Pick your lane.** Niche down—"marketing" is too broad, "B2B SaaS content strategy" is a lane.
2. **Create before you're ready.** Your first 100 pieces will be mid. Ship anyway.
3. **One platform first.** Master LinkedIn OR YouTube OR podcasting. Expand later.
4. **Consistency beats virality.** Weekly for 2 years beats daily for 2 months.
5. **Engage more than you post.** Comments build community faster than broadcasts.
6. **Repurpose everything.** One talk = clips + blog + newsletter + social posts.
7. **Build an email list.** Platforms change. Email is yours forever.
8. **Monetize with intent.** Consulting, courses, coaching, speaking—pick your model.
9. **Collaborate aggressively.** Other people's audiences accelerate growth.
10. **Your story is your moat.** No one else has your specific experience.
