# Track: Growth Marketing & Brand

| | |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Digital Marketing, Brand Strategy, Analytics, Content Marketing, Demand Gen |
| **Est. Cost** | ~$1,000 |
| **Time to Complete** | ~9-12 Months |
| **Target Degree** | B.A. in Liberal Arts, Business Administration |

**Why:** Marketing has split into two worlds: brand builders and growth hackers. The best marketers do both. This track builds the analytical chops for performance marketing (CAC, LTV, attribution) plus the creative instincts for brand storytelling. You'll graduate ready for roles from Content Marketing to CMO track.

## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Growth Marketing & Brand
**Recommended Capstone:** Google Analytics Certification + HubSpot Inbound Marketing Certification

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Google / HubSpot |
| **Cost** | Both FREE |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why these certifications?**
Google Analytics is the lingua franca of digital marketing—every marketing job expects GA proficiency. HubSpot Inbound Marketing is the foundational methodology for content-driven growth. Together, they prove you understand both the art (inbound, storytelling) and science (analytics, attribution) of modern marketing. This is your Shield—proof you can measure what matters and create what converts.

**Stack more free certs:**
* Google Ads Certification
* HubSpot Email Marketing
* HubSpot Social Media Marketing
* Semrush SEO Certification

## Recommended Association

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | American Marketing Association (AMA) |
| **Cost** | $149/year (Professional) / $49/year (Student) |
| **Website** | [ama.org](https://ama.org) |

**Why join?** The largest marketing association in the world. Access to research, job boards, local chapter events, and the Professional Certified Marketer (PCM) pathway. Student membership is a steal for networking and resources.

Also consider:
* Product Marketing Alliance ([productmarketingalliance.com](https://productmarketingalliance.com)) — Product marketing focus
* Demand Gen Report community — B2B marketing
