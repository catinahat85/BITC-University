## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Cloud Native AI Practitioner
**Recommended Capstone:** Kubernetes and Cloud Native Associate (KCNA) + Kubernetes and Cloud Native Security Associate (KCSA)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Linux Foundation / CNCF |
| **Cost** | ~$250 each ($500 total, or ~$725 bundled with training) |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why these certifications?**
The KCNA validates your foundational Kubernetes and cloud-native knowledge—essential for deploying AI workloads at scale. The KCSA adds security fundamentals critical for production AI systems. Together, they form the entry point to the CNCF certification ecosystem and demonstrate you can operate in real cloud-native environments. This is your Shield—proof you can deploy and secure AI infrastructure, not just build models.

**Certification Pathway:** KCNA → KCSA → CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator) → CKS (Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist)
