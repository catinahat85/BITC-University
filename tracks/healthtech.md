| | |
|---|---|
| **Focus** | Health IT, EHR Systems, Clinical Informatics, Healthcare Cybersecurity, Pharma Tech |
| **Est. Cost** | ~$1,720 |
| **Time to Complete** | ~9-12 Months |
| **Target Degree** | B.A. in Liberal Arts |

**Why:** Healthcare runs on technology—EHR systems, clinical applications, medical devices, pharmacy systems. But healthcare IT is different: HIPAA compliance, HL7/FHIR standards, FDA regulations, and life-or-death reliability requirements. This track builds IT fundamentals plus healthcare-specific knowledge for roles at hospitals, health systems, pharma companies, and health tech startups.

## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Healthcare Technology Professional
**Recommended Capstone:** CompTIA Healthcare IT Technician (HIT-001) + HIPAA Certification

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | CompTIA / Various HIPAA providers |
| **Cost** | CompTIA HIT: ~$250 / HIPAA: ~$25-50 |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why these certifications?**
CompTIA Healthcare IT Technician is the industry standard for healthcare IT roles—covers medical terminology, EHR systems, healthcare IT operations, and compliance. HIPAA certification proves you understand patient privacy requirements. Together, they're the baseline credentials for any healthcare IT position. This is your Shield—proof you can support clinical systems safely and compliantly.

**Level up with:**
* Epic/Cerner certifications (employer-sponsored)
* HL7 FHIR Fundamentals
* HCISPP (Healthcare Security)

## Healthcare IT Standards to Know

| Standard | What It Is | Why It Matters |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| HIPAA | Privacy & security regulations | Legal requirement; fines are massive |
| HL7 | Healthcare data messaging standard | How systems talk to each other |
| FHIR | Modern API standard for health data | The future of health interoperability |
| ICD-10 | Diagnosis coding system | How conditions are classified |
| CPT | Procedure coding system | How procedures are billed |
| DICOM | Medical imaging standard | How images are stored/shared |
| FDA 21 CFR Part 11 | Electronic records regulation | Pharma/medical device compliance |
