## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### Developer Ecosystem & Community
**Recommended Capstone:** Linux Foundation Certified IT Associate (LFCA) + Open Source Management & Strategy (LFC210)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Linux Foundation |
| **Cost** | LFCA: $250 / LFC210: FREE |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why these certifications?**
The LFCA proves technical credibility—essential for DevRel roles where engineers need to trust you. LFC210 (Open Source Management & Strategy) teaches how OSS projects are governed, funded, and grown—the business side of open source that DevRel professionals must understand. Together, they demonstrate you can ship code AND build community. This is your Shield—proof you understand both the terminal and the town hall.
