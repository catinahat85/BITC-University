| | |

## Capstone Certification

> **Note:** Capstone certifications are not ACE credit eligible and do not transfer as college credit. They are included as career-focused credentials that build your "Shield"—backing up your academic credentials with industry-recognized proof of technical competence that employers trust.

### AI Business Transformation Leader
**Recommended Capstone:** AI for Business Specialization (University of Pennsylvania)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | University of Pennsylvania (Wharton) |
| **Cost** | ~$49/month (2 months = ~$98) |
| **ACE Credit** | ❌ Not Eligible |

**Why this certification?**
Wharton's AI for Business Specialization teaches you to evaluate AI opportunities, build business cases, manage AI projects, and lead organizational change. Covers machine learning fundamentals, AI strategy, and implementation—all from a business leader's perspective. You'll learn to speak both languages: enough technical depth to work with engineers, enough business acumen to drive ROI. An Ivy League AI credential that proves you can lead transformation, not just understand technology.
